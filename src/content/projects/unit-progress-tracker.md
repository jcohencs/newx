## The problem

At iRez Solutions, tracking the rollout of IoT units across residential complexes was spread across spreadsheets and ad-hoc updates from field technicians — property managers had no single source of truth for deployment status.

## What I built

A fully integrated Notion system that tracks unit progress per property, from initial site survey through installation and connectivity verification.

## Key pieces

- A central database of properties and units with status fields tied to each stage of deployment
- Views filtered by property, technician, and deployment phase so different stakeholders see only what's relevant to them
- Integration points that keep the tracker in sync with field updates, instead of relying on manual re-entry

## Outcome

Property managers and internal stakeholders gained real-time visibility into deployment status without needing to ask the field team directly — cutting down status-check overhead and making bottlenecks visible as they happened.
