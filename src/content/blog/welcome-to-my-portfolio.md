Hey — I'm Josh. Welcome to my personal portfolio, a space I built to share my work, document my journey, and give people a real sense of who I am beyond a bullet-point resume.

I'm currently a student at the **University of Central Florida** studying Information Technology, and I've been getting hands-on experience at **iRez Solutions**, an IoT company where I help manage technical operations, build internal tools, and support field technicians working in smart living communities.

## What this site is

This is my corner of the internet. The resume page covers my experience, skills, and projects in a format that's a little more alive than a PDF. This blog is where I'll document things I'm learning, share what I'm building, and occasionally write about whatever's on my mind — whether that's a new Notion workflow, something I figured out while scraping data, a game mechanic I'm trying in Godot, or the current Pokémon TCG meta.

I learn best by doing and by writing things down. Posts here won't always be polished — they're more like working notes I'm making public. If something's useful to you, great. If not, keep scrolling.

## A bit about me outside of work

I'm big into **Trading Card Games**, particularly Pokémon TCG. I've been playing competitively for a while now and still actively build decks and track the meta. The strategy involved — resource management, reading your opponent, adapting mid-game — is genuinely one of the things that sharpened how I think through problems.

I also tinker with game development in Godot on the side, mostly as a creative outlet. There's something satisfying about building systems that interact with each other in interesting ways, and game dev scratches that itch in a way that's different from IT work.

## What's coming

I'll be posting more here as I go — project writeups, things I'm learning at iRez, notes on tools I've built, and more.
